# Request for Proposal — Food & Beverage Concession

**Issued by:** Sunset Bay Resorts, LLC
**RFP Reference:** SBR-2026-FB-014
**Date:** March 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Sunset Bay Resorts operates three beachfront properties in Florida — Miami Beach, Naples, and Key West. We are seeking a single food-and-beverage partner to operate a co-branded restaurant concession across all three resorts under an exclusive multi-year agreement.

## 2. Scope of Work
- Operate one co-branded signature restaurant at each of the three resort properties.
- Develop a new signature menu, co-created with our culinary team, unique to the Sunset Bay and partner brands.
- Exclusive food-and-beverage concession rights within resort restaurant outlets for the term of the agreement.
- Consistent brand and service standards across all three locations.

## 3. Term & Budget
- Contract term: 3 years, renewable.
- Estimated annual contract value: USD 60,000–75,000 per year.
- Pricing to be quoted in both USD and COP.

## 4. Timeline
- Proposal submission deadline: April 15, 2026.
- Target concession opening: June 2026.

Note: exact projected covers per day per property are not yet finalized and will be shared during negotiation.

## 5. Evaluation Criteria
Proposals will be evaluated on brand fit, operational capability across multiple sites, menu innovation, and overall value.

We look forward to receiving your proposal.

Director of Resort Operations
