# Ordering and Pickup

## In-store and phone orders

To place an order today, call your favorite Brasaland location or visit us directly during regular hours.

## Online ordering

Online ordering through the website is **coming soon**. When available, it will be announced on brasaland.com and in the Brasa Points app.

## Delivery

Delivery is available through the Brasa Points mobile app in participating markets. Brasa Points discounts apply to app delivery orders the same way as in-store purchases when ordered through the app.

## Pickup

Call-ahead pickup is available at all locations. Please call the location directly to confirm timing.
