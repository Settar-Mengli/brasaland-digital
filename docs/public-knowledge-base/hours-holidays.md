# Hours and Holidays

## Regular hours (all locations)

Every Brasaland location is open **Monday through Sunday, 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM** local time.

Colombia locations use Colombia time (America/Bogota). Miami locations use Eastern Time (America/New_York).

## Holidays

Hours may vary on major holidays. Check with your local restaurant by phone on the day of your visit.

## Per-location details

Individual addresses and phone numbers are listed in `locations.json`. In this portfolio demo, all 14 locations share the same weekly schedule above.
